About the Academy

Khuphuka Leadership Academy is a leadership, education and skills development academy dedicated to developing confident leaders, skilled professionals and empowered communities through transformative learning experiences.

Founded in 1996 by Tsitsi and Strive Masiyiwa during Zimbabwe’s HIV/AIDS epidemic, the foundation was established with a clear purpose: to uplift the most vulnerable and help spark hope where it was needed most.
