About Us

Khuphuka Leadership Academy is a leadership, education and skills development academy dedicated to developing confident leaders, skilled professionals and empowered communities through transformative learning experiences.

Founded in 2025 by Jennifer Mahloko and Mthobisi Masondo, the academy was established with a clear purpose: to uplift and help spark hope where it was needed most.

Our programmes are designed to support learners at critical transition points in their schooling journey, equipping them with life skills, leadership capability and academic readiness.

Why choose us

We deliver innovative learning experiences that develop leadership, build practical skills and create opportunities.

  • Every programme is built around doing: real scenarios, real tools and real feedback, so what you learn on the day is what you use the next.

  • Integrity, service and accountability sit underneath everything we teach. We develop leaders that people trust.

  • No two schools, teams or communities are the same. We shape content, pace and delivery around your context and your goals.

  • Our curricula are shaped with practitioners and employers, so the skills you build track what the workplace actually needs.
