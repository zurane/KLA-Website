Who We Are

Khuphuka Leadership Academy is a South African leadership, education and skills development academy committed to empowering individuals, strengthening organisations and transforming communities through purposeful learning experiences.

To become a leading provider of leadership, education and skills development.

To deliver innovative learning experiences that develop leadership, build practical skills and create opportunities.

What we value

  • Integrity

    We do what we say, and we hold ourselves to the standard we ask of the people we teach.

  • Excellence

    Well-prepared sessions, honest feedback and follow-through, every time.

  • Growth

    We measure our work by what changes for the people in the room after the session ends.

  • Innovation

    We keep looking for better ways to teach, and we change our methods when we find them.
